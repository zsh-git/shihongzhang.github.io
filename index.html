About

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center of Tsinghua University. I received my Ph.D. in Pure Mathematics from Nanjing University in 2025.

My research is mainly concerned with conformal geometry, conformally invariant operators, geometric inequalities and nonlinear elliptic equations arising from curvature problems.

Research Interests

  • Conformal geometry and conformal boundary operators
  • Q-curvature, GJMS operators and Paneitz-type equations
  • Sharp Sobolev and Sobolev trace inequalities
  • Higher order elliptic equations and geometric PDEs
